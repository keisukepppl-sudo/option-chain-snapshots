# Vendor Qualification Questionnaire

We need historical daily data for TQQQ, SQQQ, QQQ, and NDX from 2020 onward.

For ETF NAV/AUM/shares, please confirm whether your manual flat-file export includes:

1. Economic as-of date.
2. Original publication or available timestamp.
3. Revision/version identifier.
4. Historical vintage or revision history.
5. Raw versus corporate-action-adjusted fields.
6. Split treatment.
7. Field definitions and units.
8. Coverage and update cadence.

For daily prices, please confirm whether the export is as-published daily history or a currently revised historical series, and whether later corrections are separately identifiable.

For index data, please confirm whether NDX values include original publication timestamps, correction/revision identifiers, and raw close methodology.

We require a manual flat-file export only. No API integration is requested.
